Privacy Policy — LinkCRMKit

Last Updated: November 2026

Publisher: Yuktix Labs

Contact: yuktixlabs@gmail.com

LinkCRMKit ("we", "our", or "the extension") is built with a privacy-first, zero-server architecture. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring full compliance with data protection standards, including GDPR for users in the European Union and the United Kingdom.

1. Data We Access

When you actively visit a LinkedIn profile (https://www.linkedin.com/in/*) and open the LinkCRMKit Side Panel, the extension reads publicly visible profile fields from your active browser tab (Full Name, Headline, Company, Location, and Profile URL) strictly to populate your CRM sync form. We do not scrape private messages, connections, or password information.

2. Where Data Is Stored

Local Browser Storage: All contact records, custom notes, pipeline stages, and extension settings are stored locally inside your browser using Chrome's secure chrome.storage.local API.

User-Configured CRM Webhooks: If you choose to enter a third-party CRM Webhook URL (such as Zapier, Make, HubSpot, or Pipedrive) in your settings, contact data is transmitted directly from your browser to that endpoint when you click Sync Contact to CRM. We do not intercept or store this data on external servers.

3. Payments & Licensing

One-time Lifetime Pro license keys are processed and validated directly through our secure Merchant of Record (such as Lemon Squeezy or Dodo Payments). We do not collect, process, or store credit card details or financial information inside the extension.

4. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer user data or extracted contact records to any third party, data broker, or advertising network.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page with a revised revision date.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries, support questions, or data deletion requests regarding Yuktix Labs and LinkCRMKit, please reach out to us at yuktixlabs@gmail.com.
